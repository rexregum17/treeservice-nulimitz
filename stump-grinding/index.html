Finish the job after a tree comes down

Stump grinding chews the stump down below the soil line so you can add soil, seed grass, or plant something new. It is available on its own if a tree was already removed, or it can be added to a new removal quote.

When you request a quote, mention how many stumps you have, about how wide they are, and whether they sit close to a sidewalk, a fence, or a buried utility. The crew can tell you how far they can grind and whether the chips can stay as mulch or should be hauled off.
